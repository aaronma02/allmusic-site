Who We Are

Young musicians,
meaningful mission

All Music for Life is a youth nonprofit organization founded in Palo Alto, California. Our members are students from high schools across the Bay Area and universities including UCLA, Northwestern, Cornell, and more. We perform in hospitals, senior homes, and community centers, and also hold regular virtual concerts and busk on the streets.

We formed in 2022, performed over 30 concerts, and raised more than $6,500 for various causes.
