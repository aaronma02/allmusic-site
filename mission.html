All Music for Life

Our Mission

Music for community, comfort, and connection.

Who We Are

Changing lives
through music

All Music for Life is a youth nonprofit organization based in Palo Alto. We bring musicians together to perform in hospitals, community centers, and senior residences across the Bay Area. We also hold regular virtual concerts and busk on the streets.

Our mission is to uplift and inspire lives across our community by sharing the gift of music. We aim to deliver performances that provide comfort and hope to people across all life stages. We also strive to create a lifelong love for music and foster connections through music's universal language.

We are committed to nurturing emerging talent and demonstrating the beauty of creativity, while building bridges across generations through artistic expression. Since 2022, we have grown to over 20 members, performed more than 31 concerts, and raised over $6,000 for various causes.

"Our mission is to share music with our community and bring comfort to the people who need it." All Music for Life

What We Stand For

Our core values

01

Community Performance

We perform in hospitals, senior residences, and community centers. We also hold virtual concerts and busk on the streets regularly.

02

Youth Development

Chamber group work and mentorship that help young musicians grow their skills, take on leadership, and give back to their community.

03

Connecting Generations

We perform for audiences of all ages, from young families to seniors, and try to make music something everyone can share.

04

Charitable Impact

Our fundraising concerts have supported lung cancer research, wildfire relief, and international humanitarian aid through UNICEF.
