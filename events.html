All Music for Life

Events

We perform benefit concerts and community performances at hospitals, senior homes, and public venues, stream online performances to reach wider audiences and connect with communities, and busk regularly on the streets in downtown areas across the Bay Area.

Past Events

What we've done

Benefit Concert Mountain View, Castro Street

LA Wildfire Relief

A performance on Castro Street to support wildfire relief efforts in L.A. County. We raised funds to help with relief and recovery for those affected by the fires.

Hospital Concert Stanford Hospital

Reller Family Concert

A live performance for patients, families, and staff at Stanford Hospital. The concert gave patients, visitors, and staff a chance to experience uplifting live music and feel a sense of community.

Fundraiser Mountain View

GO2 for Lung Cancer

A benefit concert for lung cancer research and patient support. Proceeds directly supported lung cancer patients through one-on-one support, educational materials, and research funding. Attendees praised the musicians' desire to uplift and inspire.

Fundraiser Downtown Mountain View

Ukraine and Turkey-Syria Earthquake Relief

Our first concert, on Castro Street in Downtown Mountain View. All proceeds went to UNICEF to provide critical aid — including supplies, medical assistance, and relief — to children and families affected by the conflict and earthquake.
